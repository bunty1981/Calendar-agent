Description of the Service

The account owner personally invites a small number of participants and manually adds each participant's phone number before they can use the Service. A participant may text a natural-language description of a calendar event (e.g. "Dinner with Sam Friday at 7pm") to the Service's phone number. The Service creates a corresponding event on the account owner's personal Google Calendar and replies with a confirmation. Replying "UNDO" within a short window removes the most recently created event.
